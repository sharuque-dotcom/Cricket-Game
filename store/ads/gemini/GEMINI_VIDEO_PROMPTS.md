# Gully to Glory: cinematic trailer, Gemini (Veo) prompt pack

**The idea:** bring the game's own paintings to life for about 25 seconds, then show **real gameplay** before the "Play free" card. Ads that show things the game doesn't have get rejected or punished by Google, and players who feel tricked uninstall and leave 1-star reviews. Every shot here is a real scene and a real line from Chapter 1–3.

## How to make each shot in Gemini
1. Open Gemini → **Video** (Veo). Tap **+** and upload the start image named in the shot.
2. Choose **9:16 (portrait)** if Gemini offers it. If it only makes 16:9, tell me and I'll send landscape start frames instead (16:9 is useful for YouTube anyway).
3. Paste the prompt. One shot at a time. Each clip is about 8 seconds; I'll trim the best 3–4 seconds of each.
4. Not right? Generate again. Faces drifting, extra people or words appearing are the usual problems.
5. Send me the clips named `shot1.mp4` … `shot6.mp4`. I'll cut the trailer, add the real gameplay and the end card, and make vertical and square versions.

**Every prompt already includes these rules:** animate this exact image, keep the painted style and the same face, no text, no subtitles, no logos.
