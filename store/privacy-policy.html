Gully to Glory – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Gully to Glory ("the game") is a cricket story game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location. The name you type for your character, your story choices and your progress are saved only on your device and are never sent to us.

New episodes

When the game starts, it downloads the list of story episodes and any new episode files and pictures from our content server (GitHub, which hosts the files). Like any web request, this shows the server your device's IP address. No personal information or game progress is sent. GitHub's privacy statement: docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads (for example, "watch an ad to get a key"). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (such as model, operating system and app version),
  • ad interactions (such as whether an ad was shown or completed).

This information is collected by Google, not by us. Google's use of it is described in Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

The current version has no in-app purchases. If purchases are added later, they will be processed by Google Play, and we will update this policy.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It contains mature themes (debt, gambling and match-fixing as story elements). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data deletion

All game data is stored on your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes it.
