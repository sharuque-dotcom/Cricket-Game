# Coming-soon covers: 4 story teasers (matching the current covers)

These four covers go on the shelf as **Coming soon**. The game counts which ones players tap, and the most-tapped story gets written first.

## Match the existing covers
The current covers (`title`, `cover_bluecap`, `cover_bollywood`, `cover_owner`) share one look:
- **The hero or couple fills the lower two-thirds**: waist or thigh up, centred, faces large, sharp and expressive, looking at the viewer or at each other.
- **A floodlit cricket stadium in the background**, with the setting (gully, wedding, city) in between.
- **A glowing sky** in the top third: golden sunrise or pink-purple dusk. It's where the game puts the title.
- **Sparkle:** confetti, bokeh, sun rays or flash bursts.
- **Painted semi-realistic Indian-poster style:** warm skin tones, rich saturated colour, soft glow, never dark or gritty.

**How to use**
1. **Attach one existing cover as a style reference** with **+**, every time. Use `cover_bollywood` for romantic ones, `title` or `cover_bluecap` for the cricket ones. Tell it: "in exactly the same painted style as the reference image".
2. Pick **9:16**.
3. No text on the image.
4. Everyone is fictional. No real cricketers, actors, team kits or logos.
5. Send them back named `cover_banned`, `cover_groundsman`, `cover_dropped` and `cover_trade`.
