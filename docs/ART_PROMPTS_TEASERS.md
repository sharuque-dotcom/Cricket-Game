# Coming-soon covers: 4 story teasers

These four covers go on the shelf as **Coming soon**. The game counts which ones players tap (1.5.5 `book_open`), and the most-tapped story gets written first.

**How to use**
- Pick **9:16** for every cover.
- No text on the image. The game adds the title.
- Match the bright look of the current covers. If a result comes out dark or dull, attach `cover_owner` or `cover_bollywood` as a *style* reference and add "in the same bright painted style as the reference".
- Everyone is fictional. Don't name or resemble real cricketers or actors, and use no real team kits, league logos or brands.
- Send them back named `cover_banned`, `cover_groundsman`, `cover_dropped` and `cover_trade`.

**Already in every prompt:** "Vibrant, luminous digital painting, cinematic sports-drama book cover, rich saturated colours, soft glow, no film grain, no vignette, no logos, no readable text." The top 20% is kept as simple glowing sky or shadow, so the game's title sits cleanly.
