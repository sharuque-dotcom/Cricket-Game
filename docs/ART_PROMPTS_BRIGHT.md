# Gully to Glory — Prompt Pack: Bright First Impression (1.5.0)

This pack covers the 7 paintings a new player sees in their first minute, redone to match the new bright, colourful app. Everything else stays as it is.

**How to use**
- Copy one block at a time. Each prompt already includes the style and the size. Pick **9:16** every time.
- **Attach the current image with +** (it's listed under each prompt). Then the place and layout stay familiar and only the light and colour change. If the result copies the old darkness too closely, try again without the attachment.
- Send them back with the file name (for example "intro"). I'll resize them to 768×1376, compress them and put them in.
- Same story, same places, same time of day. **Night stays night**, but it becomes warm, glowing and colourful instead of murky.

**What "bright" means here (already written into every prompt)**
- Luminous and saturated: warm sunlight, golden hour, glowing lamps. Lots of colour in the sky.
- Keep the painted look of the current art. No photorealism, no cartoon.
- No film grain, no heavy vignette, no black shadows swallowing half the frame.

**Where the images get cropped**
- **Covers (B)** show in 3 places: a wide banner on the home screen (the strip from about 15% to 55% down the image), a small portrait card on the shelf (the full image) and the book page banner. **Keep faces and the main subject in the upper-middle, centred.** Titles are no longer drawn on top of the cover, so no empty space at the bottom is needed.
- **Backgrounds (A)**: characters stand in front of the bottom 70% and the dialogue box covers the bottom 25%. Put the interesting part (sky, buildings, light) in the **top half**.
