## The story

You are the national captain. After a World Cup semi-final loss and a party scandal, your image is broken. Superstar Tara Kapoor's career is too. A publicist writes a one-year contract marriage, and clause seven says "no falling in love". Her controlling mother, her ex Vivaan (who leaks the contract in three parts), your Dadi moving in (one bed, a wall of pillows), a kiss-cam, a fractured cheekbone, a London film offer, and the divorce papers she signs first. Three endings: a real wedding, best friends, or apart.
