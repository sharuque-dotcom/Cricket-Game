# Bollywood Wife — Prompt Pack (revised for the bright 1.5.0 look)

Same 25 images as before, rewritten so they pop like the new covers and the Chapter 1 paintings: luminous, colourful and glowing, with no film grain and no murky shadows.

**How to use**
- One block at a time. Pick **9:16** for everything.
- **Characters (A, B):** green background. I cut them out. Wide images are fine; I crop them.
- **Keep faces the same:** attach `char_tara` with **+** for anything with Tara in it (A1, B1–B3, D1–D3). For each expression in B, attach that person's A image.
- **For the look:** if a result comes out dull, attach one of the new paintings as a *style* reference (for example `bg_street_dusk` or `cover_bollywood`) and add "in the same bright painted style as the reference".
- Send them back with the file names, in a zip if that's easier.
- Everyone is fictional. No real actors, film studios, award logos or brands.

**Already have:** `char_tara` (her party portrait already fits the new look) and the new `cover_bollywood`. Stadium, press room, airport, overseas hotel, physio room and hospital scenes are reused from earlier books.

**Priority:** A (people) → C (scenes for Episodes 1–5) → D → B.

**The house style (already in every prompt)**
- *Characters:* "Vibrant, luminous digital painting, soft bright studio light with a warm rim light, rich saturated colours, clean game character art."
- *Scenes:* "Vibrant, luminous digital painting, game background art, soft glow, no film grain, no vignette." Night scenes stay night, but glowing and colourful instead of dark.
- *Layout:* the interesting part of a scene goes in the **top half**. The bottom 30% stays simple, because characters stand there and the dialogue box covers it.

## The story

You are the national captain. After a World Cup semi-final loss and a party scandal, your image is broken. So is superstar Tara Kapoor's career. A publicist writes a one-year contract marriage, and clause seven says "no falling in love". Then come her controlling mother, her ex Vivaan (who leaks the contract in three parts), your Dadi moving in (one bed, a wall of pillows), a kiss-cam, a fractured cheekbone, a London film offer, and the divorce papers she signs first. Three endings: a real wedding, best friends, or apart.
