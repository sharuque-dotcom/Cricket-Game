**Already have (reused):** stadium at night, dressing room, nets (`bg_academy_nets`), hostel room, brand shoot, hotel lobby, street at dusk.

**Priority:** A1 Meera → cover → A (rest) → C1–C6 (Chapters 1–5) → C7–C12 → B.

**The house style (already in every prompt)**

- *Scenes:* "Vibrant, luminous digital painting, game background art, soft glow, no film grain, no vignette." Night scenes stay night, but glowing and colourful instead of dark.
- *Layout:* the interesting part of a scene goes in the **top half**. The bottom 30% stays simple, because characters stand there and the dialogue box covers it.

## The story

You're a rookie batter from Sonpur, six days into your first season with the Mumbai Monarchs. Leaving the captain's engagement party, a girl in a silver lehenga jumps into your auto-rickshaw: "Drive. Don't ask." By morning you learn who she is. Meera Malhotra is the owner's daughter, and she's engaged to your captain, Kunal Sethi, to pay her father's 300-crore debt to Kunal's father. There's a secret phone, Gate 4 at 11 PM, the girls' team she secretly coaches, a ladder held by her 81-year-old Nani, and Kunal's plan to take the whole team. The wedding and the final fall on the same night. Three endings.
