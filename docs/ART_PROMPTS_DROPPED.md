## The story

You're a batter from Kuttanad in Kerala, 24, and the best in the domestic game. The national selectors have dropped you nine times in three years. Your Amma runs the Lakshmi, a patched-up houseboat restaurant, which has just failed its safety survey. The night the World Cup squad leaves you out again, a Gulf league agent offers two point four crore if you quit Indian cricket. At 11:58 PM a Delhi number calls: the chairman's nephew, Aryan Khurana, is injured, and you're on the 6:10 flight. In England you find Aryan secretly playing on a torn hamstring, a journalist from Kozhikode with the selection minutes, and one man who vetoed you nine times.
